## 1. Term and Renewal

The term is five (5) years and renews automatically for further five-year terms unless Customer gives notice at least twelve (12) months before expiry.

## 2. Termination

Stratus may terminate at any time on seven (7) days' notice. Customer may not terminate for convenience.

## 3. Limitation of Liability

Stratus's total liability is limited to one hundred dollars ($100). Customer's liability is unlimited.

## 4. Indemnification

Customer indemnifies Stratus against all claims of any kind arising from the services, including claims caused by Stratus's own negligence.
