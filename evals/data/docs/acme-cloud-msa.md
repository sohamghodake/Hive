# Acme Cloud Master Subscription Agreement

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme Cloud Inc., a Cayman Islands exempted company ("Provider"), and Northgate Retail Ltd ("Customer"), effective as of the date of the last signature below (the "Effective Date").

## 1. Definitions

"Service" means Provider's hosted inventory forecasting platform, including all updates Provider makes generally available. "Customer Data" means data submitted to the Service by or for Customer. "Usage Data" means telemetry, logs and statistics generated by operation of the Service. "Order Form" means an ordering document signed by both parties that references this Agreement.

## 2. Term and Renewal

The initial subscription term is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date. Thereafter this Agreement and every Order Form renew automatically for successive thirty-six (36) month renewal terms unless Customer delivers written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term. Notice of non-renewal must be sent by registered mail to Provider's registered office; email notice is not effective.

## 3. Termination

Provider may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for convenience upon fifteen (15) days' written notice to Customer. Customer may terminate this Agreement only if Provider commits a material breach that remains uncured sixty (60) days after Customer's detailed written notice describing the breach. Upon any termination, all fees for the remainder of the then-current term become immediately due, and prepaid fees are non-refundable under all circumstances. Provider will delete Customer Data thirty (30) days after termination and has no obligation to provide an export.

## 4. Fees and Payment

Customer shall pay all subscription fees annually in advance. Invoices are due net ten (10) days from the invoice date. Overdue amounts accrue a late charge of two percent (2%) per month. Provider may increase the fees at any time, by up to twenty-five percent (25%) per increase, on thirty (30) days' notice by email; continued use of the Service after the notice period constitutes acceptance. Provider may suspend the Service without liability if any invoice is more than five (5) days overdue.

## 5. Service Levels

Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Service available 99.0% of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance, which Provider may perform at any time. If availability falls below that level, Customer's sole and exclusive remedy is a service credit of five percent (5%) of the monthly fee, which must be requested within ten (10) days of the end of the affected month.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own information of a similar nature, and no less than reasonable care. The receiving party may disclose Confidential Information only to its employees and contractors who need to know it and are bound by written obligations at least as protective as these. These obligations continue for three (3) years after termination, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 7. Intellectual Property and Data Rights

Provider retains all rights in the Service. Customer grants Provider a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, copy, modify and create derivative works of Customer Data for any purpose, including training and improving machine learning models offered to other customers. Provider owns all Usage Data and all derivatives of Customer Data, including aggregated forecasts and analytics, and may commercialize them without restriction.

## 8. Indemnification

Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its affiliates from any and all claims, losses and expenses arising out of or relating to Customer's use of the Service, including claims alleging that the Service, when used as permitted, infringes a third party's intellectual property rights. Provider has no obligation to indemnify Customer for any claim.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Provider's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the one (1) month immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. In no event shall Provider be liable for loss of data, security breaches, unauthorized access to Customer Data, or any indirect, consequential or punitive damages, even if advised of their possibility. These limitations do not apply to Customer's liability, which is unlimited.

## 10. Data Location and Security

Provider may store and process Customer Data in any country in which Provider or its subprocessors operate. Provider may change subprocessors without notice. Provider will maintain security measures it considers appropriate, which it may modify at its discretion.

## 11. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Cayman Islands, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Any dispute shall be resolved exclusively by binding arbitration seated in George Town, Grand Cayman, conducted in English, and Customer shall bear the costs of the arbitration regardless of outcome. Customer waives any right to a jury trial or to participate in a class action.

## 12. General

Provider may assign this Agreement without consent; Customer may not assign it without Provider's prior written consent. Provider may amend these terms by posting a revised version on its website. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter.
