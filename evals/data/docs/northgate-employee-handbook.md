# Northgate Retail Employee Handbook (excerpt): Time Off, Remote Work and Expenses

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue paid time off at a rate of 1.75 days for each full month of service, starting on their first day. Up to five (5) unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year, but carried-over days expire if they are not used by March 31. Requests for more than three consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive ten (10) paid sick days per calendar year, separate from paid time off. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than three consecutive working days. Sick days do not carry over and are not paid out on departure.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to sixteen (16) weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child. Secondary caregivers receive six (6) weeks of fully paid leave, which may be taken in up to two separate blocks within the first year. Employees become eligible after completing ninety (90) days of employment.

## Remote Work

Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to three (3) days per week with their manager's agreement. All employees, whether remote or in the office, are expected to be available during core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone. Working from another country for more than fourteen (14) days in a year requires prior approval from HR and Legal because of tax and immigration rules.

## Equipment

Each employee receives a home-office equipment stipend of $1,200 every three years, which may be used for a desk, chair, monitor or similar items. Company laptops are refreshed every four years, and must be returned within five working days of an employee's last day.

## Travel

Flights shorter than six (6) hours must be booked in economy class. Flights of six hours or longer may be booked in premium economy. All travel must be booked through the company's travel portal so that duty-of-care tracking works.

## Expenses

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $75 per day for domestic travel and $100 per day for international travel. Itemised receipts are required for any single expense above $25. Expense claims must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the expense being incurred; late claims may be rejected. Any single expense above $500 requires approval from a director before it is incurred.

## Learning and Development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of $2,000 for courses, conferences, books and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over. Courses that cost more than $1,000 require a short written plan agreed with the employee's manager.
