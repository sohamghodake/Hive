# Brightline Professional Services Agreement

This Professional Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Brightline Consulting LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Consultant"), and Northgate Retail Ltd ("Client").

## 1. Services and Statements of Work

Consultant will perform the services described in each statement of work signed by both parties ("SOW"). Each SOW states the scope, deliverables, milestones and fees for that engagement. If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls unless the SOW expressly says otherwise.

## 2. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months. It does not renew automatically; any extension requires a written amendment signed by both parties. An SOW in progress at expiry continues under these terms until its deliverables are accepted.

## 3. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days of notice. On termination Client pays only for services performed through the termination date, and Consultant refunds any prepaid fees for work not yet performed on a pro-rata basis.

## 4. Fees and Invoicing

Consultant invoices monthly in arrears for services performed. Client pays undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days of receiving a correct invoice. Client may withhold disputed amounts in good faith while the parties resolve the dispute. Hourly rates stated in an SOW are fixed for the term of this Agreement. Travel expenses require Client's written pre-approval and are reimbursed at cost without markup.

## 5. Acceptance

Client has ten (10) business days after delivery to accept or reject each deliverable. A rejection notice must describe the non-conformity, and Consultant will correct it at no additional charge.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's Confidential Information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement. However, Consultant may share Client's Confidential Information with its affiliates, subcontractors and technology partners without restriction and without notice to Client. The confidentiality obligations in this section survive for six (6) months after this Agreement ends.

## 7. Ownership of Work Product

Upon payment in full, Client owns all deliverables and work product created specifically for Client under an SOW, including all intellectual property rights in them. Consultant retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates and know-how, and grants Client a perpetual, royalty-free license to use any of them that are incorporated into a deliverable.

## 8. Indemnification

Each party will defend and indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its gross negligence or willful misconduct. Consultant will also defend and indemnify Client against any claim that a deliverable infringes a third party's patent, copyright or trade secret, and may at its option modify the deliverable so that it no longer infringes.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Except for breaches of confidentiality, indemnification obligations, and gross negligence or willful misconduct, each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months before the claim arose. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, subject to the same exceptions. This limitation applies equally to both parties.

## 10. Insurance

Consultant maintains commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and professional liability insurance of at least $1,000,000, and will provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 11. Non-Solicitation

During the term and for six (6) months afterwards, neither party will solicit for employment any employee of the other who worked on an SOW, except through general advertisements not targeted at those employees.

## 12. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. The state and federal courts located in New York County have exclusive jurisdiction, and each party bears its own legal costs.
